## Part I　Writing

**题目：** 大学生应为未来的职业生涯做好更充分的准备

开篇句：*As requirements for job applications are getting increasingly higher, college students ought to be better prepared for their future career.*

### 写作思路

**段落结构：**
- 第一段：强调职业准备对大学生应对高要求就业市场的重要性及其双重价值。
- 第二段：从学术能力、实践经验和软技能三方面具体分析职业准备的关键要素及其作用。
- 第三段：总结职业准备的必要性，呼吁大学生主动学习以把握未来机会。

### 参考范文

As requirements for job applications are getting increasingly higher, college students ought to be better prepared for their future career. Such preparation not only gives them a competitive edge but also establishes a robust foundation for long-term professional development.

A distinguished academic record serves as the primary indicator of a student's professional potential. Furthermore, practical experience gained through internships or part-time jobs allows students to apply theoretical knowledge in real-world settings, significantly enhancing their employability. Beyond academic achievement and hands-on experience, soft skills such as communication, teamwork, and adaptability have become equally vital in today's workplace. Employers increasingly value candidates who can collaborate effectively, solve problems creatively, and adapt swiftly to changing environments.

In conclusion, as the job market grows increasingly competitive, college students must proactively build a well-rounded skill set that combines academic excellence, practical experience, and interpersonal competencies. Only by doing so can they stand out from the crowd and seize the opportunities that lie ahead.

## Part IV　Translation

**题目：** 南水北调工程

### 参考译文

China has historically faced an uneven distribution of water resources, with the south abundant in water and the north severely lacking it. To effectively address the serious water shortage problem in the north, the Chinese government implemented the South-to-North Water Diversion Project. It is a large-scale water conservancy project that allocates water resources across regions. After decades of planning and preparation, construction began in 2002. The project is divided into eastern, central, and western routes with a total length of 4,350 kilometers, benefiting a population of over 400 million. Since the project began delivering water in 2014, it has transferred a cumulative total of over 50 billion cubic meters of water northward, providing reliable water resources for people in northern regions, while also greatly improving the ecological environment of this area and promoting sustained and healthy economic development.
