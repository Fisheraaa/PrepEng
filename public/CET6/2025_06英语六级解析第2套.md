## Part I　Writing

**题目：** 正确且负责任地使用社交媒体的重要性

开篇句：*As social media is used more and more extensively, there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly.*

### 写作思路

**段落结构：**
- 第一段：概述正确且负责任地使用社交媒体的必要性。
- 第二段：详细阐述正确且负责任地使用社交媒体的具体表现及其重要性。
- 第三段：总结重申正确负责任使用社交媒体对构建和谐、包容的网络空间至关重要。

### 参考范文

As social media is used more and more extensively, there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly. While it broadens perspectives and connects people, its potential drawbacks, such as spreading misinformation and causing cognitive overload, remind us to maintain a critical mindset.

To maximize the positive function of social media, it is crucial to develop the ability to screen information effectively. Users should actively filter out low-quality content and stay focused on valuable information such as industry insight and professional knowledge. By doing so, users not only prevent themselves from wasting time on mindless scrolling but also convert social platforms into powerful self-improvement tools that foster sustained growth in cognitive abilities.

In conclusion, we should embrace social media as a tool that serves human progress rather than dominates it. With proper use, social media can become a force for connection, education, and positive change, fostering a healthier and more inclusive online environment for everyone.

## Part IV　Translation

**题目：** 粤港澳大湾区

### 参考译文

The Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area enjoys unique geographical advantages, well-developed transportation infrastructure, and rich industrial resources. It is one of the most open and economically vibrant regions in China, playing a strategically important role in the country's economic development. The Greater Bay Area is not only set to become a world-class city cluster full of vitality and an international center for science and technology innovation with global influence, but will also be developed into a high-quality living area suitable for work, shopping, and tourism. As reform and opening-up continues to deepen, the construction of the Greater Bay Area will further boost regional economic development. By 2035, the Greater Bay Area will achieve its goal of becoming a world-class bay area.
